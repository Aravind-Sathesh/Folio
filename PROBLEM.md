## Prompt: Side Hustle

Build something with open-source AI at its core: an open-weight model, an open-source agent harness or framework, local inference, or a mix. The open pieces must be what makes the project work.

Theme: build something with open-weight models or open-source AI that someone would actually pay for. Think small, specific and useful: a real problem for a real person (a freelancer, a small business owner, a niche hobby community, a friend with a tedious task). Their examples: a local invoice and receipt sorter for freelancers, a listing writer for Etsy/eBay sellers, a menu translator for a local restaurant.

## The post must cover

- What we built, who pays for it, and why
- A demo
- Why open innovation matters for this project: does running your own model keep costs low enough that the business works, keep customers' data private, allow fine-tuning for a niche a general API handles poorly, avoid lock-in to someone else's pricing? Where did open beat closed?
- Required tags: `#devchallenge`, `#hf26challenge` (added by the submission template)
- Optional but encouraged: agent session saved with DevRelay, embedded or linked
- Credit any non-trivial prior work or open-source code used

## Judging

1. Writing quality (weighted most heavily)
2. Relevance to prompt and theme
3. Creativity
4. Technical execution
5. Use of partner technology (optional)
